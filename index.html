ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Risda, an Informatics Engineering graduate from Universitas Negeri Semarang. I am interested in exploring how data and technology can be used to solve business problems, improve processes, and support better decision-making.

I have gained professional experience across data analytics, business intelligence, project monitoring, and digital solutions through roles at Astra Credit Companies, PT PLN (Persero) UP MLEB, PT MIDI Utama Indonesia, and PT Aspal Polimer Emulsindo. These experiences have helped me develop both technical capabilities and an understanding of business operations.

During my experience as a Corporate Strategic Intern at Astra Credit Companies, I contributed to the development of IMPACT, an interactive Power BI dashboard that centralizes project budgets, actual spending, projections, and benefit monitoring. By consolidating data from five separate Excel files into one master database, the solution reduced monthly data consolidation and validation time from 80 to 16 hours, representing an 80% reduction. I also contributed to project timeline monitoring, reporting automation, and the development of a data-driven project scoring and grading framework.

Beyond business intelligence, I have a background in Data Science and Machine Learning, with experience in data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, predictive modeling, and computer vision. I work with tools and technologies such as Python, SQL, Power BI, Microsoft Excel, TensorFlow, and Scikit-learn to turn data into meaningful insights and practical solutions.

I enjoy analytical problem-solving, continuous learning, and working on challenges at the intersection of data, technology, and business. I am always interested in exploring new opportunities where I can contribute my skills, gain new experiences, and create meaningful impact.
